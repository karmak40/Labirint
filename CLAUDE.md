# Labirint

Godot 4.7, GDScript, 2D top-down, all graphics procedural (`_draw()`), no sprites.

## Current direction: RTS "база на базу"

The full approved plan lives in [docs/rts_plan.md](docs/rts_plan.md). Read it before starting any RTS work. It has the architecture, milestones M0–M9 and how to verify each one.

Progress: plan approved on 2026-09-24. M0 to M8 done on 2026-09-24; M9 partly done (batched rendering, tower weakened), and optimisation is on hold until the game is tuned.
- M0/M1: `Team`, `PlayerBody.team`/`team_of()`, `Knight`→`Unit` (`scripts/unit.gd`), `Order`/`set_rally`/`set_attack_move`, `scripts/squad.gd`. Sides come from the scenes: `Player.tscn` has `team = 1`, `Knight.tscn` has `team = 2`.
- Friendly fire is off (decided with the user): `PlayerBody._nearest_target` and `hit_target_at` skip `Team.allied` bodies. NEUTRAL is nobody's ally, so the dummy still gets hit.
- M2: `NavFloor` (`scripts/nav_floor.gd`) bakes the floor from static colliders. `Pathfinder` (`scripts/pathfinder.gd`, a `NavigationAgent2D` with RVO) is shared by `Unit` and `Worker` and created in code. Without a nav region a `Unit` walks along X only, which keeps the testbed unchanged. Idle bodies give way to the crowd (`settle(wish, give_way)`).
- M3: `Economy`, `PlayerState` (economy + squad; `_claim_field` hands its economy to stockpiles of its team), `Stockpile` (banks resting `Beam`/`Rock` within `RADIUS`), `Worker` + `scenes/worker/Worker.tscn` (`job` = wood/ore/gold), `OreVein.resource_kind` with `Rock.resource_kind` for gold. Resources are banked only on delivery. Test scenes: `scenes/tests/EconomyTest.tscn`, `scenes/tests/BattleTest.tscn`.
- M4: `Building` (`scripts/building.gd`, `StaticBody2D` with the dummy's target contract, footprint collider built in code, health bar) with subclasses `Base` (`base_building.gd`, `base_destroyed(team)`), `ProductionBuilding` (`CATALOG` of kinds/costs/times, `queue_unit(kind)`, `set_rally_point`, `unit_ready`) and `Tower` (its own homing bolts, shoots bodies only). `PlayerState` claims buildings (`building.side`), and `base()`/`barracks()` claim lazily. `Unit` scans every 0.25 s with a random phase, prefers people over walls (`WALL_BIAS`), and treats a blocked attack-move goal as reached within `GOAL_SLACK`. Test scene: `scenes/tests/BaseTest.tscn`.
- M5: `MapData` (`scripts/map_data.gd`: floor, trees, veins, gold, `player`/`enemy` as `SideLayout` sub-resources with base, barracks, stockpile, towers, rally, start_workers and start_stock), `MapBuilder` (`scripts/map_builder.gd`, builds everything in `_ready`, `states[team]`/`state_of(team)`), `resources/maps/skirmish_small.tres` (900×560, mirrored left to right, fits the screen without a camera), `scenes/main/Skirmish.tscn`. Trees are 190 px tall, so keep them in the bottom strip or they cover whatever stands above them.
- M6: `GameState` autoload (`scripts/game_state.gd`: map, sides, phase MENU/PLAYING/WON/LOST, `match_ended`, and the commands `hire`/`attack_move`/`attack_enemy_base`/`rally_home`/`set_rally_point`, each taking a team; the AI in M7 must use the same ones). `scenes/ui/MainMenu.tscn` is now `run/main_scene` (decided at M6; its "Тестовый стенд" button opens `Main.tscn`). `scenes/ui/Hud.tscn` (`scripts/ui/hud.gd` builds its UI in code; `player_input_adapter.gd` maps RMB to an attack-move and Shift+RMB to the rally point), `scripts/ui/rts_camera.gd`. Gameplay ticks (`ProductionBuilding`, `Tower`, `Stockpile`) run in `_physics_process`. `Unit.leash` (200 px from its post, set only by orders). `Pathfinder._unstick` makes a stalled walker sidestep; workers spread across different sources. `Unit`/`Worker` turn off `_unhandled_key_input` (the body jumps on Space). `MapBuilder` finds `GameState` through `/root/GameState` rather than by name, so a map still builds without the autoload.
- M7: `AIDirector` (`scripts/ai_director.gd`), a child of its side's `PlayerState`, added by `GameState.register_match` for every side the human is not playing. It thinks every ~2 s with jitter (the only gameplay randomness), acts only through the `GameState` commands, and sees the enemy only through the `targets` group. Its plan: 2+2 workers first, then whichever trade a knight is short of, then waves (4, +2 each, up to 12) with retreat when spent, home defence, and a counter-attack after repelling one. The menu has an "ИИ против ИИ" mode (`GameState.spectating`; the HUD then shows both stores and hides the command bar). `ChopTree.regrow_time` and `OreVein.refill_time` (0 = never, which keeps the testbed unchanged) come from `MapData`; skirmish_small uses 40 s and 25 s. The woodcutter costs ore and the miner costs wood, so a side can't get stuck with an empty store. `Unit.HAND_BIAS`: on attack-move, units chase workers only when very close. Workers skip sources inside hostile tower range. Units and workers draw a team-coloured ring at their feet (`Team.draw_foot_ring`). `AIDirector` also looks up GameState through `/root/GameState`, because GameState creates it.
- AI vs AI check (headless, `--fixed-fps 60`): 7 of 8 matches finished on their own, between 2.5 and 11 minutes; both colours win.
- M8: `Achievements` autoload (`scripts/achievements.gd`, a `ConfigFile` at `user://achievements.cfg`, 10 achievements in `LIST`, `unlock(id)` is idempotent, `track(event, amount)` keeps running totals). It listens only to signals, starting at `match_started`: the new `PlayerBody.died` (one line in `_start_death`), `Tower.destroyed`, the human side's `Stockpile.received` and `ProductionBuilding.unit_ready`, and `match_ended`. Nothing counts in AI vs AI. `GameState.match_time` counts play time. The HUD shows an unlock toast, and the menu has an "Достижения" screen. **Any test that plays a match must set `LABIRINT_ACHIEVEMENTS=<path>`, or it writes into the real save.**
- M9 in progress. Target from the user: phones with 40+ units per side. Measurements on this PC (GL Compatibility, real frame with rendering; N knights per side fighting on skirmish_small):
  - Start: N=20 21.2 ms, N=40 39.3 ms, N=60 56.7 ms. The bottleneck was about 33 draw calls per rig figure. Simulation (targets, nav, AI) at N=60 is about 4.6 ms, so `SpatialGrid` is not needed.
  - Done: `Pen` (`scripts/pen.gd`) gathers a figure into one triangle array (1 draw call). `walker.gd` routes all its `draw_*` calls through `_w_*` wrappers and draws through the Pen when `batched` is set. `batched` defaults to `Pen.crowd_mode`, which `MapBuilder` raises in `_enter_tree` and lowers in `_exit_tree`, so the testbed draws exactly as before (4.2 ms, unchanged). In crowd mode a rig animates every other frame (`CROWD_STRIDE`, round-robin `crowd_slot`), and its hit/grab events fire on crossing a threshold, so nothing is lost. `Pen.feather` (1 px soft edge) replaces antialiasing, because GL Compatibility has no 2D MSAA (MSAA is only enabled on other renderers). Team rings are drawn only in crowd mode.
  - Now: N=20 11.4 ms, N=40 19.3 ms, N=60 27.7 ms (without feather: 8.1 / 13.5 / 19.0). What remains is CPU in GDScript: Pen tessellation (~11 ms without feather, ~19 ms with it, at 132 figures) and rig animation (~4.5 ms).
  - Tower weakened (M9, the user's decision): range 150, damage 9, every 1.4 s. The user plans that towers will later be built for resources and won't be on the map at the start. Gold is for later (the idea is weapon research and unlocking units).
  - On hold, the user's decision on 2026-09-24: game design and tuning first, further optimisation later. Options for then: quality settings in GDScript (feather off, stride 3, simplified small figures) or moving the Pen and rig maths to C#/GDExtension.

- AI difficulty and strategies (user request, 2026-09-25):
  - `AIProfile` (`scripts/ai_profile.gd`): `STRATEGIES` are balanced «Ровный», rush «Натиск» (spearmen + warriors, only spears/forging, no towers, waves from 3, fights to the last), turtle «Оборона» (4 towers early, archers/crossbowmen, first attack not before 480 s, wide home guard), and tech «Знания» (big economy, knights + crossbowmen, attacks only after chivalry + forging). `make(strategy, difficulty)` merges a strategy with a difficulty. Easy: slower thinking, 1 hire per thought, fewer workers, smaller waves, no counter-attack, only 3 studies, one tower fewer, +180 s before attacking. Hard: faster thinking, more hires and workers, bigger waves, pulls back sooner, attacks earlier. No difficulty cheats. "random" picks a strategy.

  - `GameState.ai_strategy` (default "random") and `ai_difficulty` (default "normal") go to the enemy's director; in AI vs AI both sides are random. Campaign rooms set the strategy (`ai`: forest balanced, gorge turtle, siege tech); difficulty is the player's choice.
